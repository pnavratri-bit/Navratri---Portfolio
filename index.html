02 / HOW I THINK

Research is not a step

My role sits between understanding people and shaping the product. I care about why a problem exists before deciding what should be built

01

Separate the user's actual need from the first solution that comes to mind

Use conversations, journeys, observations and usability signals to reduce assumptions

03

Turn insight into decisions

Translate what we learn into flows, priorities, information structure and product behaviour

04

Validate the experience

Prototype, test and iterate so the final experience reflects what people actually need
